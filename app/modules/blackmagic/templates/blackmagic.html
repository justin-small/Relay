How cables get here

Relay cannot see a cable. It suggests one when a Videohub output's label names a switcher input (like "ATEM 1", or the input's own name), and you confirm it; or press Add a cable and click its two ends.

Add by address

For a device the scan does not find, for example on another VLAN. Relay joins each device as one more control app and reconnects by itself. An ATEM Constellation also has a Videohub inside it: add it again as a Videohub, by its address, to route it.

Models Relay can draw

Relay picks the drawing from the model each device reports; change it in a device's Settings. A model Relay does not know yet is drawn as the nearest panel of its kind, and still connects.
