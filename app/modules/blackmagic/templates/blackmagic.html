Blackmagic

Connect Relay to Blackmagic gear on the same network: ATEM switchers (their program and preview tally shows on the camera screens at /tally, and the booth can cut and take from here), HyperDeck recorders (record, stop and play) and Videohub routers (see and change what goes where).

Relay joins each device as one more control app, and reconnects by itself. A device added from the network list is followed to a new address if its DHCP lease changes. An ATEM Constellation also has a Videohub router inside it: add it again as a Videohub, by its address, to route it.
