Planning Center

Show the service plan on panels and screens, and run Services LIVE from here.

{# Which service the Service, Team and mics and Rundown tabs show: the one pick every part of the page shares. #}

Service you are viewing

Plan and time come from Planning Center. The Service, Team and mics, and Rundown tabs show this choice: the order of service with Next and Previous, who is on each channel, and the /screen rundown.

Plan: Relay uses the plan dated today. Pick another to use it until the day ends. Service time: Automatic picks the running service, else the next one.

Next and Previous work only while the person who made the token has control, the same as a panel's press. Every press is listed under Panels.

Service types Relay follows

Relay reads plans only from the types you tick, and panels can only pick these. Untick the ones you never run. The list comes from your Planning Center account.

Panels

Panels show the plan and can press Next and Previous as the person who made the token.

Presses from panels

{# Soundboard channels on the board, each assigned to positions of any service type. #}

Channels on the board

Add the soundboard channels you want to track, then assign each one to the positions that use it. A channel can serve positions from several service types, so a mic shared by Sunday and Youth is set up once.

Add channels from the soundboard
Channels already on the board are ticked.

Older mic settings are still in use:

They list mics by position instead of channels. Move them to the board to see and change them here.

Relay gives every confirmed person in a position one of its channels, drawn at random once and then kept, so nobody changes mid-service. It only changes when someone confirms or drops out, or when you press Shuffle. People past the last channel get none.

{# The rundown (#148): /screen, what it shows, and the note fields it reads. #}

The rundown on /screen

/screen is a display-only page for the production team's monitor: the order of service with clock times, who is on which mic, the song leader and the sermon notes. It never scrolls and has no buttons, so everything is fitted to one screen.

Off by default. It shows volunteers' names, photos, mics and sermon notes, so it is available on this network only. Relay refuses it through remote access, and the panels and audience caption pages never carry any of it.

What the screen shows

Every item is fitted to the screen. The short list stays large and readable from far away.
Per position groups everyone on a team under their role.
Show one team only, such as Worship for the stage monitor.
Automatic picks whichever service LIVE is running, else the next one.
Open it in a browser on the screen's computer. Team and service time are part of the link, so each screen can have its own.

Plan files on signage screens

Copies the pictures and videos attached to the current or next plan, and to its items, into the signage media library, and can keep a signage playlist called Planning Center plan in step with them. Relay looks again every five minutes and when you press Sync now. A file is downloaded once and again only when it changes in Planning Center. Off until you switch it on. Needs the Signage module.
