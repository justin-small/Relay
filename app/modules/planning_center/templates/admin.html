Planning Center

Follow the service plan and show it on panels.

Connection

Make a Personal Access Token at api.planningcenteronline.com/personal_access_tokens and paste its application ID and secret here. Everything Relay does in Planning Center, including Next and Previous from a panel, runs as the person who made the token and only within their permissions. The secret is stored server-side in config.json, never shown again, and never written to the log, an export or a recording. Until a token is saved, Relay does not contact Planning Center at all.

Rundown

Same rules as a panel's press: Relay presses as the person who made the token, Next and Previous work only while that person has control, and every press is listed under "Presses".

Mics by position

Give a position its mics. A position with more than one confirmed person gets one mic per person, drawn at random once per plan and service time and then kept: it changes only when someone confirms or drops out, or when you press Redraw. People past the last mic get none. "Sings" lights the mic on /screen during songs; untick it for a speaking mic (a host or a pastor's headset), which is lit for everything else.

Link for a screen
