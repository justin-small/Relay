Lighting

Fire your lighting program's cues from Relay. Group the cues you want into pages; only a cue on a page can be fired from a screen, a board or a slide tag.

{# Fire cues: one page at a time, its levels, and the cues on no page. #}

Levels

Cues not on any page

Fire one once from here. Put it on a page to use it on screens, boards and slide tags.

A cue that is fired is sent once. If the connection drops, Relay does not try again, because the cue may already have happened.
