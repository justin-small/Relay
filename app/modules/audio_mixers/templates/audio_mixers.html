Audio Mixers

Your sound desks, live: channel names, mutes, faders and scenes, the sends into each mix, a watchdog for changes nobody made through Relay, and mic names from Planning Center.

Mic names from Planning Center

The next service's mics, from the Planning Center page's Team and mics tab, give each desk channel the name of the person on it. Nothing goes to a desk until you confirm.

Watchdog

Save what each desk should look like, then pick who hears when a name, a watched mute or the scene changes without Relay doing it. Their phone buzzes until they read it (Crew notifications).

I/O patch from a show file

The desk does not tell Relay what is patched to its inputs, so export the show (the .tar.gz from the desk's USB or Director) and choose it here. Relay lists each input channel's name and socket, and what the patched outputs carry, for the signal map. Avantis and dLive only; nothing is written to the desk or the file.

Desks

Relay talks to each desk over its own control protocol (Allen & Heath MIDI over TCP, Behringer OSC, Yamaha RCP). These drivers are built from the published protocols and have not been tried on a real desk yet: try them before a service. On macOS and Windows, allow incoming connections the first time Relay asks; an X32 or WING answers over UDP.
