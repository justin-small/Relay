AES67

Network audio streams Relay hears announced (AES67, as Dante devices in AES67 mode, Ravenna and Livewire send it). Choose one and Relay receives it as its sound input: no driver to install. Dante Virtual Soundcard cannot send AES67; a Dante device needs AES67 mode switched on in Dante Controller.

A stream appears when its device announces it (usually every 30 seconds) and goes after five minutes without an announcement. Relay listens to the stream's channel you pick, or all of them mixed.

Input now

Relay does not lock to the network's PTP clock. It counts lost packets and measures how fast the samples arrive against its own clock (parts per million); the drift shows after thirty seconds.

Switches

Multicast needs IGMP snooping and an IGMP querier on the switches between the sender and this computer. Without them the stream either floods every port or never arrives. A stream that is announced but silent usually means the switch is dropping multicast, or the stream goes out of a different network port than Relay listens on: set the AES67 port under Network.
