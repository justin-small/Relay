OBS Studio

Follow OBS on one or more computers: the scene, whether it is streaming and recording, dropped frames, and a preview. Switch scenes from here or from a tablet, and send Relay's captions into the stream.

In OBS, open Tools > WebSocket Server Settings, switch on the server and note the port (4455) and password. Captions reach viewers only on platforms that show CEA-608 captions from RTMP (YouTube, Twitch); they are sent only while OBS is streaming.
