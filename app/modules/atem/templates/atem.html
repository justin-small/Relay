ATEM

Connect Relay to one or more Blackmagic ATEM switchers on the same network. Their program and preview tally shows on the camera screens at /tally, and the booth can cut and take from here.

Switchers

No switchers yet. Find one on the network below, or add it by its address.

Relay joins each switcher as one more control app, and reconnects by itself. A switcher added from the network list is followed to a new address if its DHCP lease changes.

Find on the network

Lists Blackmagic gear that announces itself on this network: switchers, HyperDecks, Videohubs, cameras and more. Only ATEM switchers can be added here for now.
