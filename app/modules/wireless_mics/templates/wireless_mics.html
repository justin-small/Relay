Wireless mics

Battery, signal, audio level and mute for Shure wireless receivers, and a list of mics and in-ear packs with no network. Each one can be tied to a Planning Center position, so its card shows who is scheduled there.

Not yet proven on real receivers. The Shure part was written from Shure's published command strings and tested against a stand-in receiver. Check each reading against the receiver's own display before you rely on it.

Receivers

Shure QLX-D, ULX-D and SLX-D receivers, reached on TCP port 2202. Relay must be able to reach the receiver's address.

Manual mics and packs

Gear with no network. It shows a name and a position and no readings.
