Open Sound Meter

Listen to the level an Open Sound Meter install broadcasts over UDP, and show it on boards and the status feed next to Relay's own meter. In a Sound level or Spectrum widget, choose "Read from: Open Sound Meter". Copy the address and port from Open Sound Meter's own network settings.
