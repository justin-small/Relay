UniFi

Relay reads the church's UniFi console, read-only: the internet connection, switches and their ports, Wi-Fi, NDI and Dante traffic, firmware, the console's change log, and who is on the network.

Trust the console's certificate

The console uses a certificate this computer does not know, which is normal for a UniFi console. Check that this fingerprint matches the one your browser shows when you open the console's address, then trust it. Relay connects only while the console shows this certificate.
