Remote crew

Crew who may use the intercom from outside the building, through Cloudflare. Local phones and local audio never depend on this.

Who may connect remotely

Only people marked here can connect from outside, even with a valid account. They sign in at the install's remote address with their own Relay login and get the same channels and rights as on site.
