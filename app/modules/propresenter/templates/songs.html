Songs

Build a song, check how the slides will look, and send it to ProPresenter. ProPresenter cannot make a song over its network, so the Relay Helper on the ProPresenter computer opens the file for it.

You can read songs here but not send them.

Paste lyrics and chords

A line that is only a section name (Verse 1, Chorus, Bridge) starts a section. A blank line starts a new slide. A line starting with / is the translation of the line above it. Chords as [G], or on a line above the words.
