Slide view and lobby

The slides ProPresenter has up, as pictures with the countdown and video progress over them, and a rotation that steps a ProPresenter announcement playlist for lobby screens.

Slide view

Lobby announcements

Relay steps through a ProPresenter playlist by itself, one slide at a time. ProPresenter does not say which playlists are announcement playlists, so pick the one your lobby screens show. It does not start on its own: press Start. If you put something else on the announcements layer, the rotation waits and carries on when you clear it.
