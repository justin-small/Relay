Slide view and lobby

Three separate ways to get ProPresenter’s slides in front of people who are not at the ProPresenter computer. Each has its own settings and works without the others.

1

Slide view

A live picture of the slide that is up and the one coming next, with the countdown on it, for anyone who cannot see the screen: the sound desk, the green room, a tablet backstage.

ProPresenter→Relay→A web page at /stage
2

Lobby rotation

Relay moves a ProPresenter announcement playlist along by itself, one slide every few seconds, so lobby screens that ProPresenter drives never sit on one slide. Use this when your lobby TVs are fed by ProPresenter. It does not start on its own: press Start.

Relaypresses Next onan announcement playlist→ProPresenter lobby screens
ProPresenter does not say which playlists are for the lobby, so you pick.
If something else is put on the announcements layer, the rotation waits and carries on when it is cleared.
Not running.
3

Signage copy

Copies each slide of a playlist into Relay’s signage as a picture, so TVs on Relay signage can show the announcements without ProPresenter. Use this when your lobby TVs are signage screens, not ProPresenter outputs. Needs the Signage module.

ProPresenter playlistcopied every two minutesRelay signage→Signage TVs
