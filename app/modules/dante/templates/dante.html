Dante

The Dante devices Relay finds on its Dante network: stage boxes, Dante Virtual Soundcard and Dante Via. Each appears once by its Dante name, with the transmit channels it announces and whether its control port answers. This page only looks; it changes nothing on a device. Works with Dante devices.

A control port that does not answer may still be reachable: Relay's check is a first version that has not yet been tried on real Dante gear. Between the main network and a Dante VLAN, the router has to let UDP through to the control port (4440 for most devices, 24440 for Dante Via).
