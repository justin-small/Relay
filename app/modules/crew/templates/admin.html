Crew app

An installable page for the people who serve. They sign in with a Relay account made on the Users page (the Member role is enough) and see what their role may use.

The page

A phone on the church network checks in by itself. Where a location is set, a person away from the network can tap Check in inside this radius (20 to 5000 metres, 150 if blank). Relay only compares the position with this one and keeps no coordinates.
One site per line (its subdomains count too). Resources can embed a page only from these; any other address is a plain link. Leave it as it is to allow YouTube, Google Docs and Drive, and Vimeo.

Checklists

Page the crew

A page buzzes each person's phone, and buzzes again for up to two minutes until they confirm on their own link. People turn pages on from the crew page on their phone.

Call times

Page a Planning Center team on a schedule: minutes before each of today's service times, or at a set day and time. It reaches the people on that team serving today who have a crew account, and buzzes like any page. A call time that Relay misses by a minute is skipped, never sent late.

Opening it on a phone

Crew open /crew on this machine's HTTPS address (the one the operator panel uses). The phone has to trust Relay's certificate first, or the browser will not install the page or allow the microphone. The page is never reachable through remote viewer access.
