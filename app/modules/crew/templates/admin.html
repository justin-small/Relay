Crew app

An installable page for the people who serve. They sign in with a Relay account made on the Users page (the Member role is enough) and see what their role may use.

The page

Opening it on a phone

Crew open /crew on this machine's HTTPS address (the one the operator panel uses). The phone has to trust Relay's certificate first, or the browser will not install the page or allow the microphone. The page is never reachable through remote viewer access.
