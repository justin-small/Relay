Crew app

An installable page for the people who serve. They sign in with a Relay account made on the Users page (the Member role is enough) and see what their role may use.

Call times

Page a Planning Center team on a schedule: minutes before each of today's service times, or at a set day and time. It reaches the people on that team serving today who have a crew account, and buzzes like any page. Its time also says when someone counts as late on the live board. A call time Relay misses by a minute is skipped, never sent late.

Wi-Fi check-in

With the UniFi module on, a person is checked in when one of their devices joins the church network. People add their own phone from the crew page; you can add one by its MAC address or the hostname the network shows.

The UniFi module is off, so nobody is checked in by Wi-Fi.

Check-in by location (optional)

A phone on the church network checks in by itself. Where a location is set, a person away from the network can tap Check in inside this radius (20 to 5000 metres, 150 if blank). Relay only compares the position with this one and keeps no coordinates.
