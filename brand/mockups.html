Relay logo: the Bubble R

Pick a version and every surface below redraws with it. Colours come from the app as it is today: black, off-white, and the green that already means live.

The chosen mark: a separate upright and a caption bubble with two lines, together reading as R.

The same R drawn as an outline, in the stroke the wordmark is written in, so mark and name read as one hand. Lightest of the three; at 32 pixels and under the stroke thickens and the caption line drops out.

The upright and the bubble touch, so it reads first as a solid capital R and second as a bubble. Boldest, and the same drawing works at every size.

The upright becomes a person and the bubble shows three dots: someone speaking, words arriving. Most literal about what Relay does; the R is a little less obvious.
