Crew app redesign: three options

Each option covers the crew phone (Today or Home, Chat, Checklists, Resources, Intercom, Activity), the page, join and offline screens, and the operator's Crew app page. Switch views with the tabs at the top of each.

A. Tabs across the top

Phone: Today, Chat, Checklists, Resources, Intercom as a top tab bar. Operator: Today, Paging, Checklists, People and phones, Setup.

B. A home screen and a live board

Phone: a "what do I do now" home and a bottom bar; your settings in a sheet. Operator: a live board of every team with paging beside it, setup in tabs.

C. Me, my team, talk

Phone: three tabs. Operator: one tab per Planning Center team holding its people, call time, checklists, files and leads.
