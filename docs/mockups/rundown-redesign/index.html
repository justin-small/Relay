A · The hour to scale

Each item is as tall as its minutes, so the 32-minute sermon fills half the column. Lines between the plan lane and the real one slant where the service slipped. On the micboard, each mic has its own strip of the hour, lit for the songs it is drawn for.

B · Channel strips

Mics look like strips on the WING: the number, a scribble strip with the name, and the colours from your own snapshots (MC green, vocals yellow, band blue). A strip's lamp is lit when that mic is drawn for the item on now. The order of service is laid out like the desk's scene list.

C · Read from the platform

Black, very large, and timing said in words. It shows only what is wrong with the roster, not the whole roster. The micboard is just numbers and first names. Spoken mics are dimmed while a song is on.
