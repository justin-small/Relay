The rundown on /screen

/screen is a display-only page for the production team's monitor: the order of service with clock times, who is on which mic, the song leader and the sermon notes. It never scrolls and has no buttons, so everything is fitted to one screen.

Off by default. It shows volunteers' names, photos and sermon notes, so it is available on this network only. Relay refuses it through remote access, and panels and audience caption pages never carry any of it.
