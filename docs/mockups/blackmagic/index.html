Blackmagic redesign: three options

All three draw each device from a model library (ATEM Mini Extreme ISO, ATEM Television Studio HD8 ISO, HyperDeck Studio HD Plus, Videohub 20x20 and Smart Videohub 12x12): the front with the buttons Relay can press lit and clickable, the back with every connector showing signal, the device's label, details on hover and an operator note. Buttons Relay cannot press are dimmed and say why.

A. One tab per device

Each device gets a tab with its front and back stacked, a connector panel and the device's own details. Closest to today's page and Planning Center.

B. The rack

Every unit drawn in a rack (desktop switcher on the shelf), turned round as one to see the back. Click a unit to control it in the side panel. A Wiring tab (from C) draws the cables between the back panels; a table of every connector holds the notes. Chosen.

C. Signal flow

Live: all front panels as one control surface, with the camera-to-recorder chain on top. Wiring: back panels with the cables drawn between them.
