Network redesign: three options

Today Network map and Network are two sidebar pages of stacked sections. All three options make them one Network page with a full-width tab bar like Planning Center and Blackmagic, and fold today’s Network page (ports and traffic) into a This computer tab. The data is made up but has the shape /api/admin/network/map returns.

A. Tabs by what you look at

Map · Devices · Switches · This computer · Sources. Closest to today’s page, split up: the map with a side panel, a filterable device table, and one switch at a time drawn front-on with its port table.

B. The closets (the Blackmagic rack, for network gear)

Racks · Map · Ports and notes · This computer · Find and add. Every switch drawn in the closet it hangs in; highlight NDI, Dante, PoE or problems and the other ports dim. Click a port for what’s plugged in and your note. Ports and notes is the table for tracing cables, and renames a device for Relay only. Chosen.

C. Health and AV traffic

Overview · Video and audio · Map · Devices · Setup. Opens on what to fix before Sunday (slow links, PoE budget, offline cameras), then who sends NDI and Dante to whom and how full each uplink is. This computer and Sources sit under Setup, with crew alerts.
