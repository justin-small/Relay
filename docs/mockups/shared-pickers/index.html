Shared port, channel and route pickers

Three ways to look, each drawn on the real pages: Network cables (port), NDI router and Audio bus (route), Intercom rooms and Planning Center mics (channel). Same data in all three.

A. Searchable dropdown

The select you have today, upgraded: a popup that searches, groups by gear and shows kind and link state. Routes stay one line per destination. Smallest change; fits every form as it is.

Open ANDIAudio busNetwork

B. Browse drawer and crosspoint matrix

The field opens a side drawer: gear on the left, its ports or channels on the right. Routes become a matrix (destinations down, sources across, one dot per route), the way a broadcast router looks. Best with many sources; widest.

Open BNDIAudio busNetwork

C. Tiles and patch board

The field expands in place into a grid of tiles, no popup, so it works on a phone. Routes are a patch board: arm a source, tap the destination. Friendliest for volunteers; takes the most room.
