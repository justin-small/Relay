Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Relay runs on your church's own computer. What it hears, the accounts you make in it and the services you connect to it stay on that computer. We do not receive them.

This policy covers the Relay app and this website, relaychurchsystems.com. "Relay", "we" and "us" mean Relay Church Systems. "You" means the church using Relay and the people who use it there.

This website

The website has no sign-in, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs (such as your IP address and browser) to deliver and protect the site. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address when you visit. If you email us at hello@relaychurchsystems.com, we keep the email to reply to you.

What stays on your computer

Relay is installed on a computer at your church. Everything below is stored there, in Relay's own data folder, and is not sent to us:

  • The accounts you create for your team: names, usernames, roles and hashed passwords, and a log of sign-ins and changes kept for 90 days.
  • Your settings, and the keys and sign-ins for services you connect. These are encrypted with a key held in the computer's own keychain.
  • Transcripts, only if you turn recording on. Relay never records audio.
  • Pictures, slides and other files you add for screens and signage.

Because this data is on your computer, your church controls it. You can delete any of it from Relay, or delete Relay's data folder.

Services you connect

Relay talks to other services only when you connect them, using your church's own account with that service. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

  • OpenAI, for captions and translation. While capture is running, Relay sends the room's audio to OpenAI under your church's OpenAI account and shows the text it returns.
  • Planning Center, to read your service plans, people and schedules, and, if you choose, to put your live stream's YouTube link on the Church Center episodes you pick.
  • YouTube, to show whether your stream is live and, if you turn it on, to schedule your live streams. See the next section.
  • Facebook, to show the live status of your church's Page, when that feature is available.
  • Equipment on your network, such as ProPresenter, OBS, lighting and cameras. That traffic stays inside your building.

YouTube and Google

When you press Connect for YouTube, you sign in with Google and approve Relay's request to see, edit and manage your YouTube account (the scope youtube.force-ssl, or youtube when your church uses its own Google app). Accounts connected before scheduling existed have the read-only scope youtube.readonly and can only be read. Relay uses this access only to:

  • read your channel's name, so you can see which channel is connected,
  • read whether your channel is live, the live broadcast's title, and when it started,
  • read the number of people watching, while you are live,
  • read your upcoming live broadcasts and your channel's stream keys' names (never the keys themselves),
  • only if you turn on scheduling: create the upcoming live broadcasts you set up in Relay, with the title, description and privacy you chose, and attach them to the stream key you picked.

Relay shows this to your production team inside Relay. It never uploads videos, and never changes or deletes a video or broadcast it did not create. If you link a Planning Center Publishing episode, Relay sends that episode the broadcast's public watch address (youtube.com/watch?v=…) and nothing else.

Google sends the sign-in back to Relay's cloud (cloud.relaysystems.dev, below), which uses Relay's app key to turn it into the access Relay needs and passes that to your church's computer. Relay's cloud holds it only until your computer collects it, at most 15 minutes, never logs it, and also renews it for your computer when it runs out. On your computer it is stored encrypted. We never read your YouTube data. We do not sell it, share it, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models. Relay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Relay uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's use of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

To stop Relay reading YouTube or scheduling streams, press Disconnect in Relay, which asks Google to revoke Relay's access and deletes the stored sign-in, or remove Relay at your Google account's connections page. Either one works on its own.

Relay's cloud

A few optional features need a small service we run, cloud.relaysystems.dev, on Cloudflare.

  • Remote access lets people outside your building open your captions page. When you turn it on, your Relay computer sends us an enrollment code, the port it serves viewers on and its Relay version, and we give it a web address. Visitors then reach your computer through Cloudflare. We keep the address while you use it and for up to 90 days after, and you can release it at any time.
  • Connecting YouTube and Facebook: when you press Connect, the service sends your sign-in back to Relay's cloud, which exchanges it for access to your account and hands that to your Relay computer. We keep it only until your computer collects it (at most 15 minutes) and when renewing it, and never log or store it otherwise.
  • Remote intercom lets crew talk from their phones over the internet. Their audio passes through Cloudflare's real-time service to reach your Relay computer. It is not recorded, and we do not receive it.

Updates

The desktop app checks for new versions at relaychurchsystems.com, which points to the release on GitHub. That request shares only your IP address and the app version, as any download does.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.
  • We do not collect usage analytics from the Relay app.
  • Relay is for churches to run their services. It is not directed at children.

Removing your data

See how to delete your data. If you want us to delete something we hold, such as your emails to us or your remote access address, email hello@relaychurchsystems.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects what Relay sends off your computer, we will say so in the app's release notes.
