Then it is one button.

The audio is captured on the computer running Relay, so nobody in the room is asked for a microphone.

    Connect your soundboard

    Send a feed from the soundboard to the computer's audio input and pick it in Relay.

  2. 2

    Press Start

    Or add a schedule and Relay starts and stops by itself, every Sunday or on a single date.

  3. 3

    Share the link

    People open it on their own phone and choose captions, a translation, or both side by side.

Captions on the wall, no phone needed.

A small touch screen for a wall, a desk or the stage. It joins your Wi-Fi, finds Relay by itself and follows the live text. Scroll back to reread, then tap Jump to live.

  • Captions, one translation, or two feeds side by side
  • Song lyrics and timers from ProPresenter
  • Today's order of service from Planning Center
  • Alert buttons for the band and the tech team

Runs on the Elecrow CrowPanel Advance 7.0". Updates install from the Relay admin page.

Works with the tools already in your booth.

Every integration is a module you switch on. While it is off, Relay never contacts that product.

ProPresenter

Relay follows the current slide and sends lyrics, timers and stage messages to your panels, screens and overlays. A view-only switch keeps it from changing anything.

Planning Center Services

Show today's service on a panel: the current and next item with timers, song keys and who is running LIVE. Allow a panel to move the service on, or keep it read-only.

It runs in your building, on your computer.

Desktop app
Install it on Windows, macOS or Linux. It sits in the tray, can start at login and updates when you choose.
Or a box in the rack
Run it as a service on a small Linux server or a Raspberry Pi with no screen attached.
Locked operator panel
Settings are served over HTTPS only and need a sign-in. The link you give the room cannot reach them.
Accounts for your team
Give each volunteer their own sign-in and a role that fits what they need to do.
No audio kept
Relay does not record audio. Transcripts are saved only if you turn recording on.
