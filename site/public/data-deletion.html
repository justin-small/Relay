Delete your data

Updated November 16, 2026

Relay keeps your data on your church's own computer, so you can remove it yourself. Nothing here needs us.

Disconnect YouTube

In Relay, open the YouTube module's settings and press Disconnect. Relay asks Google to revoke its access and deletes the stored sign-in.

You can also do it from Google, even if the Relay computer is off: go to your Google account's connections page, choose Relay and remove its access.

Disconnect Facebook

Once Relay's Facebook feature is available: in Relay, open the Facebook module's settings and press Disconnect. Relay deletes the stored sign-in.

You can also remove Relay from Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, select Relay and choose Remove. If you choose to delete past activity there too, Facebook removes what Relay could read.

Other services

Remove the key or sign-in from the module's settings in Relay. For OpenAI, you can also delete the API key in your OpenAI account, so it stops working everywhere.

Remote access

In Relay, open Remote access and press Release this address. Your web address is removed from Relay's cloud at once. An address that has gone unused is removed automatically after 90 days.

Everything on the computer

To remove your team's accounts, settings, transcripts and files, uninstall Relay and delete its data folder. If you are not sure where that folder is on your computer, email us and we will tell you.

Ask us

If you emailed us, or want us to remove anything else we hold, write to hello@relaychurchsystems.com. We will confirm when it is done.
